# Landing page and public website

**Scope:** `/` and the public pages (`/how-it-works`, `/for-contributors`, `/for-organisations`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/login`, `/register`), with the shared header and footer.
**Mode:** Persuade for `/`, `/for-contributors`, `/for-organisations`; Read for the rest.

**Audience:** hackathon judges and ecosystem reviewers first; contributors and organisations second.
**Job:** understand in seconds what Atesta does and who stands behind a record, then check one.
**Action:** start a record, or run the digest check on the sample record.
**Proof:** a working in-browser check on a clearly labelled sample record. No partners, statistics, testimonials or real attestations are shown.
**Constraints:** British spelling; must not feel cold or institutional; light and dark themes; logo is a white check on a blue rounded square; no status by colour alone.

**History:** the first direction ("The Credits", ink and paper on one axis) was rejected by the user. This replacement came from Dribbble research and a safer-register roll; the user chose it from three full-colour options and asked for dark mode and the checkmark logo.

## Direction contract

THESIS: People and their work lead, shown as real product cards: a profile timeline, a record you can check. Refuses the text-heavy editorial page and the neon web3 dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white canvas, white cards with 28px radius and one soft shadow, deep ink text, a mint highlight on one headline word, colourful organisation marks, pill buttons and status chips, Bricolage Grotesque headlines with Figtree body. Dark theme mirrors it on deep blue-black. Logo: white check on a blue rounded square.

STORY: The visitor sees a person's confirmed work at a glance, understands that organisations confirm it, checks a record themselves, and starts their own.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, the headline "Your work, confirmed by the people who saw it." with "confirmed" highlighted, a short lead, Start your record and Check a sample record buttons, and three promises. Right, a sample profile card with a three-entry timeline and status chips, labelled as a sample.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION AND MOTION: the record check: edit the role, recompute, and the result flips from Match to No match; hero content rises once on load.
